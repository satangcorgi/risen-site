Questions

What if my Bible isn't with me?

After a little while, Risen offers a photo instead: a screenshot of the verse from a Bible app or website works. If that isn't possible either, you can type the verse. There's no snooze.

What does the alarm need?

Permission to set alarms, so it rings in silent mode and Focus. Notifications are optional; with them on, Risen keeps reminding you if you leave before finding the verse.

Does Risen upload my photos?

No. Risen reads the page on your phone. If you allow online help, it sends only the words it read, never the photo, when your phone can't match a page. A small picture of your page stays on your phone for your Book, and you can turn that off.

Which Bibles work?

Any printed Bible in any translation, a Bible app or a website. Risen shows the verse in the Berean Standard Bible, World English Bible or King James Version, all public domain.

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

Cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your Apple Account. Restore Premium from the Premium page or Settings in Risen. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your first alarm keeps its verse, other alarms still ring as plain alarms, and every verse you read stays in your Book.
