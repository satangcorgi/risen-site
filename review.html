Philippians 4

Gratitude for the Philippians' Gifts

10Now I rejoice greatly in the Lord that at last you have renewed your concern for me. You were concerned, but you had no opportunity to show it.

11I am not saying this out of need, for I have learned to be content regardless of my circumstances.

12I know how to live humbly, and I know how to abound. I am accustomed to any and every situation, whether well fed or hungry, whether having plenty or having need.

13I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength.

14Nevertheless, you have done well to share in my affliction.

15And as you Philippians know, in the early days of the gospel, when I left Macedonia, not one church partnered with me in the matter of giving and receiving, except you alone.

16For even while I was in Thessalonica, you sent me aid again and again when I was in need.
